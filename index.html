Privacy policy

Mia Nixta sto Palermo (Μια Νύχτα στο Παλέρμο) · Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short: the current version of the game collects no personal data. It plays offline in Narrator mode, has no account, no ads, no purchases and no analytics, and sends nothing to us. This page also lists what later versions will collect, so you know in advance. When that changes, we update this page before the new version is released.

Who we are

The game is made by [DEVELOPER NAME], [CITY], Cyprus (“we”). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact for privacy questions and data requests: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

What the current version does

Everything you enter stays on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • your options (sound, voice, language, keeping the screen awake);
  • your saved game setups (players, roles, timers, Katina's name);
  • the player names you type for a Narrator game, and the game in progress so you can resume it;
  • that you confirmed you are 14 or older.

We never receive this data. You delete it by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

The narration voice is your phone's own text-to-speech engine (for example Google's). The app hands it the narrator's lines, which contain no personal data apart from the name you may give to Katina. The engine is part of your phone and follows its provider's privacy terms.

Google Play may send crash and performance reports about the app to us (Android vitals) if you allowed this in your phone's settings. These are collected by Google and do not identify you to us.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for anything not listed here.

Where the data is kept (EU region)

Our game server (Supabase) runs in a data centre in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany or a similar EU region). Some providers we will use, such as Google, Apple and RevenueCat, may process data outside the EU. They do so under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Providers (processors): Supabase (server and sign-in), Google (Google Play, Google sign-in, AdMob), Apple (App Store, Sign in with Apple), RevenueCat (purchase checks).

Deleting your data from inside the app

When accounts arrive, Options → Account → Delete account erases your profile, display name, progress, items and game history from our server. Records of purchases are kept only as long as the law requires. Options → Account → Export my data gives you a copy of your data.

Today there is no account: clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to your data, a copy of it, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to its use. Write to [SUPPORT EMAIL]; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy).

Age

The game is for players who are 14 or older (the age of digital consent in Cyprus) and asks you to confirm this the first time you open it. It is not designed for children. If you believe a child under 14 has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to our server travels encrypted (HTTPS). Each player can read only their own secret game data, and the app holds no secret keys.

Changes

We update this page before a version of the game that collects new data is released, and change the date at the top.
