Privacy policy

Mia Nixta sto Palermo (Μια Νύχτα στο Παλέρμο) · Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: Simple mode with real cards plays offline and sends nothing to us. Party mode, Online mode and Simple mode with cards on phones run on our game server in the EU: they use a guest account (no email, no password), the name you type in a room and the game itself, and room data is deleted within 30 days after the room closes. If you choose to, you can link a Google account (we then keep its email address) and set a display name; your level, XP and gold are kept with your account. You can export your data and delete your account inside the app. There are no ads, purchases or analytics yet. This page also lists what later versions will collect, so you know in advance. When that changes, we update this page before the new version is released.

Who we are

The game is made by Philippos Mesina, Larnaca, Cyprus (“we”). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact for privacy questions and data requests: enpotatoes@gmail.com.

What the current version does

In Simple mode with real cards everything you enter stays on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • your options (sound, voice, language, keeping the screen awake);
  • your saved game setups (players, roles, timers, the Saboteur's name);
  • the player names you type for a Simple mode game, and the game in progress so you can resume it;
  • that you confirmed you are 14 or older.

We never receive this data. You delete it by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app. The app also remembers the last name you used in a room, the room you are in (so you can go back to it), the players you blocked, and a copy of what your account unlocks (for example VIP), so Simple mode keeps it without a connection. It also remembers which guide pages you read or skipped and which practice nights you finished; these are never sent anywhere.

Party mode, Online mode, Simple mode with cards on phones, and your account

When you create or join a room, or open the Account screen, the app talks to our game server and it processes:

Reports you send about other players, and reports about you To keep rooms safe. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in moderation. Up to 12 months.

Like any internet service, our server provider sees your IP address while you play; its sign-in service uses it to limit how many guest accounts one device can make. We do not keep it with your game. Limits on how often an account can try room codes or send choices are kept for a day.

The narration voice is your phone's own text-to-speech engine (for example Google's). The app hands it the narrator's lines, which contain no personal data apart from the name you may give to the Saboteur. The engine is part of your phone and follows its provider's privacy terms.

Google Play may send crash and performance reports about the app to us (Android vitals) if you allowed this in your phone's settings. These are collected by Google and do not identify you to us.

The download page and the app file are hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages and GitHub Releases), which sees your IP address like any website and follows its own privacy terms. We do not learn who downloads the game. The app's “Share the link” button opens your phone's share menu; the app sends nothing to us. To tell you when a new version is out, the app downloads a small file from the same GitHub site (version.json, the newest version number) when it starts and when you come back to it, at most once an hour. Like any web page, GitHub sees your IP address; the app sends nothing else, and nothing reaches us.

Where the data is kept (EU region)

Our game server (Supabase) runs in a data centre in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany or a similar EU region). Some providers we will use, such as Google, Apple and RevenueCat, may process data outside the EU. They do so under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Providers (processors): Supabase (server and sign-in), Google (Google Play, Google sign-in, AdMob), Apple (App Store, Sign in with Apple), RevenueCat (purchase checks).

Deleting your data from inside the app

Options → Privacy and your data → Delete account (also on the Account screen) erases your account, the link to Google, your profile, display name, progress, items and game history from our server; your name in other players' past games is replaced by a number. Records of purchases are kept only as long as the law requires. Options → Privacy and your data → Export my data gives you a copy of everything our server holds about you. You can also write to us and we do it for you.

Simple mode with real cards needs no account: clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything it keeps. Signing out leaves your account on our server, so you can sign in again; deleting it is the way to remove it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to your data, a copy of it, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to its use. Write to enpotatoes@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy).

Age

The game is for players who are 14 or older (the age of digital consent in Cyprus) and asks you to confirm this the first time you open it. It is not designed for children. If you believe a child under 14 has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to our server travels encrypted (HTTPS). Each player can read only their own secret game data, and the app holds no secret keys.

Changes

We update this page before a version of the game that collects new data is released, and change the date at the top.
